Questions

The honest answers.

Does Bindlore work with Kindle / DRM books?

No — on purpose. Bindlore refuses DRM-locked files. It converts DRM-free files you own (many stores let you download EPUB/PDF), public-domain titles, and your own documents and scans. Converter for content you have the right to use — not a DRM workaround.

Where do my books go? Does Bindlore see them?

Nowhere off your machine. Parsing, OCR, and skill-building run inside your browser. There is no Bindlore server for your book content. Your files never leave your device.

Do I need an OpenAI or Anthropic key?

Not for the core skill export. Optional extras (summaries, glossary, Anki) use your own key, stored only on your device. Requests go straight from your browser to the provider — never through Bindlore.

Do I need an account?

No account. No sign-up. Install the extension. Drop a book.

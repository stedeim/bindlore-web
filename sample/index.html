SKILL.md

Excerpt of the skill file. It tells the agent when to open the book, and how to cite a section instead of paraphrasing from memory.

---
name: meditations
description: >
  Cite Marcus Aurelius, Meditations, when the user asks about Stoic
  practice or this book. Quote only passages in the reference files.
---

# Meditations

## Citation style

Name the work, book, and section, and point at the reference file:

> Meditations, Book II, §I (`references/book-02.md`)

Do not invent quotations. If a passage is not in `references/`,
say it is not in this sample.

## Reference files

- references/book-02.md — Book II, §I
- references/book-04.md — Book IV, §II, §III
- references/book-05.md — Book V, §II, §III

Chapter reference

Excerpt of references/book-02.md. A full export keeps one file like this per chapter, with the section markers Bindlore found in the source.

# Meditations — Book II

**Cite as:** Meditations, Book II, §I (`references/book-02.md`)

## §I

Remember how long thou hast already put off these things, and how
often a certain day and hour as it were, having been set unto thee
by the gods, thou hast neglected it. It is high time for thee to
understand the true nature both of the world, whereof thou art a
part; and of that Lord and Governor of the world, from whom, as a
channel from the spring, thou thyself didst flow: and that there is
but a certain limit of time appointed unto thee, which if thou shalt
not make use of to calm and allay the many distempers of thy soul,
it will pass away and thou with it, and never after return.

The other sample chapters are in the zip: Book IV on doing nothing at random, and on retiring into yourself; Book V on setting imaginations aside.
